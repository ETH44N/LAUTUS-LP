What is Lautus, exactly?

An AI editor for long-form video. You give it a recording, like a talk, a podcast or a webinar, and it gives you back vertical shorts: the strongest moments, reframed to 9:16, with captions and cleaned-up sound.

What kind of recordings work best?

Anything where people talk: podcasts, keynotes, interviews, panels, webinars, lectures and livestreams. If a moment would make sense to someone who missed the rest, Lautus can find it.

Do I still get a say in the edit?

Always. Lautus proposes the cuts. You keep, trim or drop them before anything is exported.

Where can I post the shorts?

Anywhere vertical video goes. Every short is 9:16, so it's made for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, and works just as well on LinkedIn.

How do I start?

Paste a link to one of your videos at the top of this page (or upload a file), sign in with Google and pick a plan. Lautus starts on your video right away; the first shorts are ready in a few minutes.

How much will it cost?

Starter is $49 a month for 30 videos with the standard edit. Pro is $199 for 100 videos and adds the premium editing style, trial reels, variations and content analysis. Scale is $499 for 300 videos, and Enterprise is sized to your team. See pricing.
