Which one is right for you.

Both turn long videos into vertical shorts. They are built for different people.

  • Pick Opus Clip if…

    you want the lowest monthly price, a free plan to try things, and you're happy to pick clips, tweak templates and fix captions yourself.

  • Pick Lautus if…

    you want to stop editing. You film, Lautus delivers shorts in your look, with your headline style and your music, and you post them.
