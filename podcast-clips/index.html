Clip my podcast

Built for two mics and one long hour.

Podcasts are the hardest videos to clip by hand. That's why Lautus starts there.

  • Both speakers, framed

    Host and guest, side by side or on separate cameras. The vertical frame follows whoever is talking. Lautus learns your face and voice by itself, so there's nothing to set up.

  • The moments that stand alone

    Lautus listens to the whole episode and keeps the answers, stories and lines that make sense to someone who missed the rest. No clips under 8 seconds.

  • Captions and a headline

    Word-timed captions for people watching with the sound off, and a headline written as you or to your viewer, in the language of the episode.

  • Your show's look

    Pick an editing style and its music once. Every clip from every episode looks like it came from the same show.

  • Clean sound

    Levels evened out and dead air trimmed, so each clip starts on the line that hooks.

  • Ready to post

    Vertical 9:16, captioned and mixed, for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
